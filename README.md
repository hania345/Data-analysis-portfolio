📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

Overview

This project presents an interactive "Sales Analytics Dashboard" built in Microsoft Power BI using a sales dataset. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs), geographical analysis, monthly sales trends, product/service performance, and deal size distribution.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate data visualization, business intelligence, and dashboard development skills by transforming raw sales data into meaningful insights.


Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing countries and territories.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Understand sales distribution across deal sizes.
- Identify high-performing products/services.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

Dashboard KPIs

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Total Sales** | Total revenue generated from all sales |
| **Average Price** | Average selling price per product |
| **Total Orders** | Total number of customer orders |

Key Insights

🌍 Territory Performance
- EMEA** generated the highest sales revenue.
- North America  ranked second.
- APAC and Japan contributed a smaller share of total sales.

📅 Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales remained relatively stable during the first half of the year.
- Sales increased significantly toward the end of the year.
- November recorded the highest monthly sales, indicating strong seasonal demand.

### 🌎 Country Performance
- The United States generated the highest sales.
- Spain and France were among the strongest-performing European markets.
- Sales were distributed across multiple countries, showing a broad international customer base.

🍩 Deal Size Distribution
- Medium-sized deals accounted for the largest share of total revenue.
- Small deals were the second-largest contributor.
- Large deals represented the smallest portion of sales.

📦 Service Performance
- A small number of services generated a significant portion of total revenue.
- This suggests that focusing on high-performing products/services could further improve business performance.

Business Recommendations

- Increase marketing efforts in high-performing regions such as EMEA and the USA
- Investigate the factors contributing to the strong sales performance in November and apply similar strategies throughout the year.
- Promote top-performing services while evaluating the performance of lower-selling offerings.
- Continue targeting medium-sized deals, as they contribute the majority of revenue.
- Explore opportunities to grow sales in lower-performing regions.
